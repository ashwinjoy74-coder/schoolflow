The rules

Rules SchoolFlow never breaks

These are hard constraints in the solver, not suggestions. If your requirements make them impossible, SchoolFlow tells you what to look at instead of handing you a broken week.

  • No teacher in two rooms at onceEvery teacher has at most one lesson in any period.
  • No free periods for studentsEvery division has exactly one lesson in every period of the week.
  • Weekly periods add upEach subject gets the periods you asked for. If a division's total doesn't fill the week, extra periods are shared fairly across its subjects.
  • One subject per teacherEach teacher keeps to their own subject, across every class they take.
  • Edits stay consistentA hand change that would double-book a teacher is refused before anything is saved.

FAQ

Questions schools ask

What do I need to get started?

A list of every class division's subjects, the teacher for each, and how many periods a week it gets. SchoolFlow comes with a complete sample school (classes 1–12, divisions A–D) so you can try it before entering your own.

Can I use a Saturday or a shorter week?

Yes. Choose anywhere from 2 to 7 school days and up to 12 periods a day. SchoolFlow checks that the week has enough room before it starts.

What happens if a timetable isn't possible?

The check step explains what needs fixing, such as a row with a missing field, a teacher given two different subjects, or a teacher booked for more periods than the week has. If the solver still can't find a timetable within the time limit, it says so and keeps your existing workbook untouched.

Can I change a lesson after generating?

Yes. Click a lesson on any class timetable to swap the subject or teacher. SchoolFlow only offers choices that don't clash and updates the teacher's sheet and counts at the same time.

How long does generating take?

The 48-division sample school solves in a few seconds. Large schools or tightly packed weeks can take longer; the search time limit is adjustable in Settings (up to 4 minutes online, 20 minutes in the desktop edition).

Where is my school's data kept?

Online, your requirements and timetables are saved in your own browser on your device. When you check or generate, they are sent to the SchoolFlow server, worked on for a moment, and not kept. To move to another computer or keep a backup, download the CSV and workbook. In the desktop edition, everything stays beside SchoolFlow.exe.
